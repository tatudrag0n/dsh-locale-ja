### s0566 — low
EN: Closed
JA: 閉じました
FIX: 終了
WHY: This is a terse status label next to its own pair partner "Closing" (s0567 「閉じています」), and 「閉じました」 reads as a report of a completed action by the user rather than the item's state; 「終了」 also avoids reusing 閉じる, which the brief fixes for the Close control.
